### 4.2 Standby AI Governance Strategies
- **`ClaudeAutoApproveGovIdStrategy`**: Standby automated Government ID verification and approval using **Claude Sonnet Vision** (`claude-sonnet-4-6` primary + `claude-sonnet-5-5` automatic failover via `IClaudeMessagesClient`) + shared `IGovIdAuthenticityEvaluator` forensics rules, failing closed (`Approved = false`) when offline or invalid.
- **`ClaudeAutoDispatchResponderStrategy`**: Multi-objective responder dispatch combining **Haversine proximity ($0.45$)**, **unchosen-unit fairness rotation bonus ($0.35$ -- prioritizing responder units that have not yet been chosen over repeatedly chosen units)**, and **active workload ($0.20$)**:
  $$\text{Score}(u) = 0.45 \cdot S_{\text{Haversine}}(u) + 0.35 \cdot S_{\text{FairnessRotation}}(u) + 0.20 \cdot S_{\text{Workload}}(u)$$
